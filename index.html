The method

One method. Different plumbing.

Our approach hasn’t changed as the client roster has widened — only what we feed the platforms. In both models the job is the same: give the platform the commercial truth it cannot see by itself.

Lead generation

A lead isn’t a lead until your sales team qualifies it. We connect your CRM and import offline conversions, so the platforms learn which enquiries actually became customers — and bid toward those.

Enquiry types are segmented, call tracking separates genuine conversations from accidental clicks, and reporting follows spend through to pipeline.

Bidding optimises toward qualified pipeline and revenue — not form fills.

Ecommerce

Revenue and profit are not the same target. We feed product-level margin data back into the platforms, so bidding chases the products that actually make money rather than the ones that simply sell.

For pharmacy and repeat-purchase categories, repeat order rates and lifetime value feed in too — because the first order is rarely where the margin is.

Profit on ad spend, not return on ad spend.

The principle is identical in both. Platforms optimise brilliantly toward whatever you tell them to value — and by default they can only see clicks and conversions of equal weight. Our job is to correct that picture with your commercial reality.

Where it breaks

Most performance problems aren’t the platform’s fault. They’re structural.

When campaigns underperform, people blame the channel. The real causes are usually poor conversion data, structures that ignore commercial reality, and buying journeys the platforms simply cannot see.

Conversion data treats everything equally

Most accounts weight every conversion the same, so platforms pick the cheapest over the most valuable. We assign real values — pipeline value for lead gen, margin for ecommerce — so bidding chases profit.

Different margins, one-size-fits-all campaigns

A £20,000 contract cannot be bid like a £200 service, and a 40% margin product cannot be bid like a 4% one. We split by service line or product margin and prioritise accordingly.

A buying journey the platform can’t see

Buyers research for weeks. If the account only tracks last click, the journey is invisible. We track micro-conversions, CRM stages and offline sales, then feed the signals that predict revenue.

Budget goes where it’s easiest, not where it converts

Default structures ignore differences by location, service line and product. We allocate budget around where you can actually convert profitably.

Demand created that the business can’t serve

Missed calls and unbooked appointments never appear in a platform report but decide whether spend becomes revenue. We align targeting and scheduling with real capacity.

Strong campaigns, weak landing journeys

A slow page or a mismatch with the ad undoes good media buying. We work the whole journey — message match, speed and the next step being where people expect it.
